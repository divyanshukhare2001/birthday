I Remember This 📸

♡

You probably don't remember
this day as clearly as I do.
It was so simple, nothing
special happened.

But the little moments
that day—those are some of my
favourites.

You were laughing at
something stupid I said,
we were sharing ice cream,
and for a while, nothing
else in this world
mattered like this.

⌁

There are prettier pictures of us.
But somehow, I love this one more.
